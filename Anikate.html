From the field to the workstation

My training began with field mapping in Odisha, Jharkhand and Purulia, and grew into mineral exploration, remote sensing and geospatial analysis. I am currently an Intern-Geologist at MMPL Pvt. Ltd. in Bhopal, where I prepare exploration proposals, interpret multispectral, SAR and hyperspectral data, and draw geological cross-sections in AutoCAD.

My M.Sc. dissertation was a multi-criteria geospatial and computational assessment of groundwater suitability in the urban aquifer systems of the Northern Yamuna Floodplain, NCT Delhi. I also publish and present on groundwater, geomorphology and ML, and I build the Python tools I use.

I am open to geology roles in GIS and remote sensing, mineral exploration and hydrogeology, and I am willing to relocate.
